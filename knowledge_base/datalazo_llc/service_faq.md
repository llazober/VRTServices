# Datalazo LLC — Service FAQ

## Q: What format does Datalazo OCR support?
A: Datalazo OCR supports single and multi-page PDF bank statements, JPEG/PNG check images, scanned statement documents, and CSV transaction exports.

## Q: How does QuickBooks Online Integration work?
A: Datalazo integrates directly via Intuit QuickBooks OAuth 2.0. Once connected, transactions extracted from statement OCR are mapped to your Chart of Accounts and exported cleanly as Journal Entries (GJ).

## Q: How do I check project or service status with Datalazo LLC?
A: Type your Datalazo Customer Reference code (e.g. `CUST-1002`) into the chat assistant to get instant status updates regarding your software setup or data processing pipeline.
