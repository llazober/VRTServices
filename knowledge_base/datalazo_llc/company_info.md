# Datalazo LLC — Company Overview & Services

## About Datalazo LLC
Datalazo LLC is an enterprise software development and OCR data automation company. We specialize in OCR extraction tools, accounting automation software, QuickBooks integrations, and DigitalOcean Cloud Storage architectures.

## Core Software Products
1. **Datalazo Bank Statement OCR Extractor:** High-speed AI vision & OCR extraction for bank statements and checks.
2. **Datalazo CRM Client Portal:** Multi-tenant customer management, document storage, and automated client email workflows.
3. **QuickBooks Online Sync Engine:** Direct OAuth integration exporting journal entries and mapped chart of accounts.
