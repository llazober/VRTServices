# VRT Services — Service Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Q: How do I check the progress of my tax return or onboarding?
A: You can type your Customer Reference Code (for example: `CUST-1001` or `1001`) into this AI assistant chat window at any time! The AI will query live database records and show your current checklist status, completed tasks, and pending action items.

## Q: What is Customer Reference Code (CUST-XXXX)?
A: Every customer at VRT Services is assigned a unique reference code starting with `CUST-` (e.g. `CUST-1001`). This reference code is included in all emails sent to you from VRT Services.

## Q: What is Form 8879 and why do I need to sign it?
A: IRS Form 8879 (IRS e-file Signature Authorization) authorizes VRT Services to electronically file your federal tax return on your behalf. IRS regulations require a signed Form 8879 before any return can be transmitted to the IRS.

## Q: How are monthly bank statements processed?
A: VRT Services uses automated OCR technology to extract transaction data, dates, check numbers, debits, credits, and descriptions from PDF/image bank statements directly into QuickBooks Online (QBO).

## Q: What documents do I need to provide for business tax returns?
A: For corporate and business tax filings, please provide:
1. Year-end Bank Statements (Jan – Dec)
2. Year-end Credit Card Statements
3. Form 1099-MISC / 1099-NEC / 1099-K forms received
4. Payroll summaries (Form 941 / Form 940)
5. Chart of Accounts & prior year tax return copies
