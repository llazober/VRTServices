# IRS Tax Publications — General Income Tax & Form 8879 Guidance

## IRS Filing Deadlines (Federal Income Tax)
- **Individual Tax Returns (Form 1040):** April 15 (or next business day if April 15 falls on a weekend/holiday). Extension available to October 15 via Form 4868.
- **S-Corporation (Form 1120-S) & Partnership (Form 1065):** March 15. Extension available to September 15 via Form 7004.
- **C-Corporation (Form 1120):** April 15. Extension available to October 15 via Form 7004.
- **Quarterly Estimated Tax Payments (Form 1040-ES):**
  - Q1: April 15
  - Q2: June 15
  - Q3: September 15
  - Q4: January 15 of the following year

## IRS Form 8879 — E-Signature Requirements
IRS Form 8879 is the declaration and signature authorization for an electronic return. Key guidelines:
1. **Timing:** The taxpayer MUST sign Form 8879 BEFORE the ERO (Electronic Return Originator) transmits the return to the IRS.
2. **Identification:** E-signatures must use knowledge-based authentication (KBA) or secure verified client portal authentication.
3. **Retention:** The tax preparation firm must retain signed Form 8879 records for a minimum of 3 years from the return due date or filing date.

## Common Small Business Deductions (IRS Pub 334 & Pub 535)
1. **Home Office Deduction (Form 8829 / Schedule C):** Qualifying space must be used exclusively and regularly for business. Standard simplified method: $5 per square foot (up to 300 sq ft, max $1,500).
2. **Vehicle / Mileage Expenses:** Standard IRS Business Mileage rate ($0.67 per mile in 2024 / $0.68 in 2025/2026) or actual vehicle operation expenses (gas, insurance, maintenance).
3. **Professional & Legal Fees:** Accounting fees, legal advice, software subscriptions, and tax prep fees directly related to business.
4. **Meals & Entertainment:** Business meals with clients or employees are 50% deductible if ordinary, necessary, and business is discussed. Entertainment expenses are non-deductible.
